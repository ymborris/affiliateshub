📖 Where do I find these metrics?
WarriorPlus: Affiliate → Offers → open an offer → stats table (Sales, Conv, Visitor value, Avg sale, Refund, Pulse).
JVZoo: Affiliates → Find Products → Stats column (Sales, Conv, EPC, Avg price, Refund).
ClickBank: Marketplace → Gravity, Avg $/sale, Initial $/sale, Rebill, Avg %/sale.
Digistore24: Marketplace listing → commission %, conversion, cancellation rate.
Universal: the product price + your commission is enough — estimates fill the rest (conservatively).
